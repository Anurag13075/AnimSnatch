Everything you need to steal like an artist.

AnimSnatch injects a powerful extraction engine directly into your browser, capable of dissecting the most complex modern web apps.

Snatch entire components

Don't just copy the HTML. Mode 1 scours the page's stylesheets to extract the exact CSS rules that apply to your selected element and its children. It even auto-formats to React JSX.

const Card = () => (
  <div className="snatched-card">
    <h3>Beautiful UI</h3>
  </div>
);

Download hidden assets

Can't right-click save that image? Wrapped in 5 divs? Mode 3 digs through the DOM to find the raw `img`, `svg`, or `background-image` and downloads it instantly.

Extract @keyframes

Stop trying to guess easing curves. Click an animated element, and we'll extract the precise CSS transition values and `@keyframes` code.

Built-in Capture History

Never lose a snippet. The extension saves your 10 most recent captures locally to your browser. Open the popup to view and re-copy them at any time.
